# Business Services Strategy

## Executive Summary

This document outlines our strategic approach to cloud and infrastructure services, emphasizing practical on-premise solutions and client-focused problem-solving over traditional cloud migration funding programs.

## AWS MAP Funding Concerns

**Position**: We do not agree with AWS Migration Acceleration Program (MAP) Funding approach.

**Rationale**: 
- MAP Funding often pushes clients toward cloud-first solutions that may not align with their actual business needs
- One-size-fits-all migration approaches can overlook existing infrastructure investments
- Client autonomy in technology choices should be prioritized over vendor-driven incentives
